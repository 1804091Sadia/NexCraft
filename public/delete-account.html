Delete your NexCraft account

You can delete your account and personal data yourself at any time.

  1. Log in to NexCraft (website or app).
  2. Open Settings from the menu.
  3. Choose Delete my account, enter your password and type DELETE.

Deleted immediately: your login, profile, CV, photo, applications, saved jobs, enrollments, certificates, badges and notifications.

Kept, without your name: messages you sent to other people (shown as "Deleted user"), and a security record that the deletion happened.
